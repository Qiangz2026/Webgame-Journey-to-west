HOW TO PLAY THIS GAME

1 Age Reminder

This game is recommended for players aged 12 and above.

2 Navigation

Guide Xuanzang on his westward journey. Help potential mentees overcome their dilemmas by thoughtfully inviting them to join your team.

3 Chapters

Interact with Wukong, Bajay, and Sandy in their respective chapters.

Your final Team Trust Score will determine your ending.
